BUILD ONE

  1. STEP 01

    Open the presets

    In StarNet, open BUILD MODE, then PRESETS. Scroll to COMMUNITY MAPS: it shows this same gallery.

  2. STEP 02

    Build it

    Pick a map and press USE, then confirm. Your current layout is backed up first, and RESTORE PREVIOUS puts it back. Your agents and conversations stay.

  3. STEP 03

    Staff it

    A map arrives with its rooms, furniture and conveyors, but nobody at the desks and no step instructions. You choose who works each step.

SHARE YOUR STATION

Built a station you're proud of? Put it in the gallery. Every StarNet player can build it, with your name on it.

  1. STEP 01

    Link your station

    Link StarNet to a free StarNet account: STORE › LINK STATION. Maps are shared from a linked station so that each one has an author.

  2. STEP 02

    Share it

    In BUILD MODE › PRESETS, press SHARE TO COMMUNITY. Name it, say how you want to be credited, and send it. A picture of your station goes with it.

  3. STEP 03

    A person looks at it

    Every map is checked before it appears here. You see its status on your account's MAPS tab: waiting, approved, live, or sent back with a note.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

What does a map carry?

The layout only: rooms, floors, walls, furniture, conveyors and how junctions route. It never carries the sharer's crew, step instructions, project folders, spending limits or connections. StarNet strips those when a map is shared, strips them again when it is published, and a third time when your station builds it.

Can a map change what my agents do?

No. A map has no instructions in it, so it can't tell your agents anything. Your station checks the gallery's signature and every file's fingerprint before it builds a map, so a tampered copy is refused.

What about props I made?

Made props stay on the station that paid for them, so they are left out of a shared map. If your station has some, its gallery picture is the floor plan rather than a picture of the station, so it never shows furniture other players won't get.

Who owns a map I share?

You do. You share it under CC BY 4.0, which lets anyone build and change it as long as they credit you. The name is yours too: only you can send an updated version of it.

Can I use a map file without the gallery?

Yes. Every map has a download link. In StarNet, PRESETS › IMPORT A BUILD… opens the file through the same checks.
