Keyboard shortcuts

// every key the app actually binds — nothing aspirational

This list is read off the shipped code. If a key isn’t here, the app doesn’t bind it. Most things in StarNet are clicks by design; the keys below are the ones worth memorising.

Anywhere in the station

Toggle fullscreenF11
Close the open window (inside a text field, blurs the field first)Esc
Leave CINEMA camera modeEsc
End a Live Voice sessionEsc
Advance a dialogue beat (also skips the typewriter)Space / Enter
Move focus between controls inside a windowTab / Shift+Tab
Text size bigger / smaller / back to AUTOCtrl+= / Ctrl+− / Ctrl+0

BUILD MODE

These are live only while BUILD MODE is open and you are not typing in a field. Open it from the bottom bar: ⚒ BUILD › BUILD MODE. There is no hotkey to open BUILD MODE itself.

SELECT — click to select · drag a machine onto another to belt them · drag a selected piece onto open floor to move it0
ROOM — click to place, drag to size1
HALLWAY — click to run, drag to size2
SURFACE — click a room to lay this deck3
DELETE — click anything; drag across a belt to clear the run5
PROPS — pick a prop, then click the deck to place it6
BELT — click one machine, then another7
COPY — click a room or prop to copy it, then click to stamp copies8
CONVEYOR LINES — choose a ready-made line, then click clear floor to place it9

Conveyors tab: the hand

On this tab, keys 1–8 replace the ROOM–COPY keys above: BAY, INBOX, OUTBOX, SPLITTER, MERGER, JOINER, FILTER, LOOP. Press the held piece’s key again to put it down. 0 and 9 work on every tab.

On a Mac, Cmd works wherever Ctrl is listed. R flips a belt’s corner while dragging and turns a paste ghost (Shift+R turns back). Drag a placed belt’s middle to branch it; Ctrl+drag it to reshape it through a point between the same machines. R and M also turn and mirror supported props.
