Ad variants are only worth writing if you could learn something from the result. Five variants that differ in every dimension teach nothing when one wins — the whole method is isolating the lever.

## Method
1. **Pin the audience and the ONE promise** before writing. Who is scrolling, what they already believe, and the single claim the ad has to land.
2. **Look at what is actually running** with web_search / web_fetch — competitor ads, the platform's own ad library, the angles that keep reappearing in the niche. Note the as-of date; ad norms move fast.
3. **Choose the lever you are testing** — the hook, the promise, the format, the proof, or the call to action — and hold everything else constant across the set. Say which lever each variant isolates.
4. **Write hooks that stop the scroll on substance:** a specific number, a named objection, a contrast, a direct callout of the audience. Vague aspiration does not stop anyone.
5. **Match the ad to the landing page.** A hook the destination does not immediately confirm buys an expensive bounce; flag any mismatch before the spend.
6. **Respect the platform's rules.** No claims about personal attributes, no before/after promises where they are banned, no fake urgency or fake social proof — a rejected or banned account costs more than a weak ad.
7. **State the read.** What result would mean the lever mattered, and what sample would make it real rather than noise.

## Rules
- **Never invent proof** — no made-up testimonials, results, ratings, or "as seen in".
- **Never write a claim the Commander cannot substantiate.** In paid ads this is a legal exposure, not just a taste issue.
- **One lever per variant, and label it.** An unlabelled variant set produces an unreadable result.
- The Commander approves and places every ad; you draft and never spend.

## Output
The variant set grouped by the lever each isolates, the reasoning behind each hook, the landing-page match check, then what result would count as a real signal.

*Needs the DISH (live ads and platform rules) and the CABINET (saved variants).*
